| DEC-006 | Accepted | Warehouse identity comes from the Berlapak snapshot at kecamatan level ("bypass by kecamatan"): shipping rates are computed by kecamatan, so no street address is stored (slice S90). (1) Each catalog offer's default warehouse is its items' Berlapak origin warehouse, one 99ribu warehouse per supplier warehouse (bundles never mix origins; a stock item has exactly one origin). (2) The committed dataset (`99ribu.catalog/v2`) carries an opaque origin key per offer and a warehouse list with generic names `Gudang <Kecamatan>, <Kota>` (numbered `Gudang <Kecamatan> 2, <Kota>` when two supplier warehouses share a kecamatan) plus province/city/district only; never the supplier's warehouse name, warehouse id or `address_id`, nor supplier/brand names (consistent with DEC-003/DEC-005). The key is the first 32 hex characters of SHA-256(`99ribu:berlapak-warehouse:<supplier warehouse id>`), stored as the unique `app.warehouse.origin_key` for idempotent mapping; the raw id stays in the host snapshot. Migration `0030_warehouse_origin` is expand-only (nullable `province`, `district`, `origin_key` plus checks), safe on existing rows. Processing time, cutoff and operational hours are not copied: the domain models them only as the owner-approved, MFA-gated `warehouse_calendar` policy, which each origin warehouse still needs before fulfillment deadlines apply. (3) `catalog:import` creates missing origin warehouses (one audited `catalog.warehouse.create` each), reuses existing ones by key, and refuses before any write a stored origin whose name/location differs or that is inactive (an operator decision, never rewritten); offers are bound or rebound through ordinary product revisions and guarded republication. (4) DEC-005 (4) single-warehouse stock is superseded: `catalog:initial-stock` receives each item into its origin warehouse, and only while the item has no stock history in any warehouse. A database already stocked in one import warehouse transitions with `catalog:rehome` (read-only dry run by default; `--apply` creates missing origins and moves available units through audited warehouse transfers create, dispatch, receive; reserved/quarantined units stay; reruns converge and resume open transfers) followed by `catalog:import`; immutable history is never edited and HPP, being per stock item, does not move. (5) Every active warehouse in the cart's clock scope is a quotable origin (the same set the publication guard accepts as default warehouses), so a published offer is never unquotable for lack of a fixture mapping or kecamatan. Simulated rates band origin vs destination: same kecamatan < same city < same province < inter-province (`district`/`local`/`province`/`cross` bases in the shipping-local fixture); names compare case-insensitively without merging `Kota`/`Kabupaten`; rows without province/district compare by city. Real courier adapters still map the origin kecamatan to the provider's area id (AutoLaris `/ongkir` takes a numeric area id); a pickup address for real waybill/pickup booking is a separate provider-gate decision, not needed for rates. | Owner decision 2026-10-23 |
| DEC-007 | Accepted | Consignment selling with every shipment booked under 99ribu's own Mengantar account; fulfillment mode is decided per origin warehouse (slice S91; future gated S94–S96, S98). (1) Business model: 99ribu is part of Berlapak; goods are bought from the owner's own Berlapak sellers outside the platform and ship from the seller's warehouse, i.e. the DEC-006 origin warehouse of the offer. The platform keeps no seller payable or seller settlement; component HPP stays a contribution cost only (OD-12). Physical stock reservation/consumption (ADR-0002) stays per origin warehouse. (2) Shipping: Mengantar only, always on 99ribu's own Mengantar account — the resi comes from us, never from the seller. AutoLaris is dropped for shipping (DEC-006 (5)'s "AutoLaris `/ongkir`" note is superseded; AutoLaris is payments only, DEC-008). Checkout uses rate checking ("cek ongkir") only; local rates stay simulated and the live Mengantar estimate adapter is gated (S95). All Mengantar contract facts (endpoints, flags, pickup rules, tracking, COD/balance behaviour) are unverified until the issued merchant contract and one sandbox call are recorded. (3) Manual path (now, while pickup data is incomplete): a paid prepaid order or a confirmed COD order whose parcel warehouse is in manual mode enters the admin queue "Perlu dipesan kurir"; the operator books the shipment in 99ribu's Mengantar dashboard (pickup at the seller's warehouse; the address is known outside the system), optionally records the Berlapak purchase reference on the parcel, then enters courier + resi, which marks the parcel shipped (physical handover: stock consumed once; the buyer sees courier and resi). A wrong resi is corrected with a reason; every version stays in the audit history. The packing deadline (from the `warehouse_calendar` processing time/cutoff, where configured) is the resi deadline; a warehouse without a calendar keeps working without a deadline and shows `calendar_missing`. (4) Automated path (future, S94): when a warehouse's pickup profile is complete and the Mengantar API flag is on, the system books through the Mengantar API with the registered pickup address. The per-warehouse pickup profile (private, owner/ecommerce_admin only) holds street address, PIC name, PIC phone and an optional pinpoint (only for instant/same-day); readiness is `complete` when street address, PIC name and a valid PIC phone exist. Street address, PIC name and phone are personal data: never in public responses, the committed catalog dataset, logs, audit details or exports; changes are append-only revisions with actor, reason and time. (5) Mode per warehouse = `mengantar_api` only when the profile is complete AND the flag is on; otherwise `manual_dashboard`. The flag stays OFF until S94 ships a verified adapter, so every warehouse is manual today and "complete profile + flag off" stays manual. Both paths coexist; a parcel's path is pinned by its first action (a resi entered manually is never re-booked; an API/simulator booking never enters the manual queue). The local carrier simulator stays usable for manual-mode warehouses as the automated stand-in for existing suites (owner confirmed). (6) Tracking: because every resi belongs to 99ribu's account, a worker-polled `TrackingAdapter` reads the account's own order tracking for shipped manual parcels — simulated locally (S91), live Mengantar gated (S98; the tracking contract is per-merchant and unverified). Provider statuses (picked up / in transit / delivered / failed) map onto the existing shipment/delivery states; `failed` is a failed delivery attempt and not terminal (a later delivered is still accepted); only delivered is terminal and records the buyer receipt, which starts H+10 commission maturity and the return window. When automation is unavailable an operator records the status from the Mengantar dashboard (audited, reason and evidence required); automated and operator observations share one idempotent, monotonic status history. Delivery is never COD collection proof. Return-to-sender of a manual parcel is a documented follow-up (operator action into the existing returns/custody flow); the operator `delivered` fallback is owner/ecommerce_admin only. A resi belongs to one live manual parcel per courier, and a COD parcel appears on one Mengantar statement only. (7) COD: Mengantar collects COD on 99ribu's account and remits through the Mengantar balance/settlement to 99ribu's bank — not through the seller. This reuses the existing provider COD/settlement model (S28 collection receipts, S30 settlement reconciliation; SRC-MENGANTAR-BALANCE: delivered pending COD is not cash until reconciled). Until a Mengantar settlement adapter exists, finance records each Mengantar COD settlement statement from the dashboard (reference, evidence, per-parcel collected amounts, Mengantar deductions, net received) and it posts through those journals: per-parcel collection `provider_receivable`/`buyer_funds_liability`; cash `company_cash`/`settlement_clearing`; Mengantar shipping charge and COD fee as `carrier_expense`; matching by the existing reconciliation decision. Expected vs received is flagged, never silently accepted: a parcel amount different from its collectible (merchandise + buyer shipping + DEC-009 COD fee once S92 lands) opens a COD recovery case; gross ≠ net + deductions is `SETTLEMENT_TOTAL_MISMATCH`; expected net = collected − Mengantar shipping charge (when netted) − Mengantar COD fee. (8) Future gated slices (outside local acceptance): S94 Mengantar pickup address registration + API booking, S95 live estimate, S96 Berlapak stock sync, S98 live Mengantar tracking. | Owner decision 2026-10-23 |

| DEC-009 | Accepted | COD fee is borne by the buyer at 3.33% (slice S92). Supersedes spec 11's "company bears payment surcharge" for COD only; AutoLaris VA/QRIS fees stay store-borne (DEC-008). (1) Rate is a versioned value in the company `payment` policy (`cod_fee_bps` = 333; a policy kind separate from the commercial policy so catalog publications are unaffected; manual-bank accounts live in the same policy), changed only through the normal reauthenticated policy flow; a quote freezes the rate in force and the order keeps it. (2) Per COD parcel: base = parcel merchandise after merchandise reductions + parcel buyer shipping (quoted shipping after shipping subsidy); fee = ceil(base × bps / 10000) in whole IDR; order COD fee = sum of parcel fees; parcel COD due = base + fee. (3) Disclosed as a separate line "Biaya COD (3,33%)" on checkout, quote, receipt and order page, included in what the courier collects. (4) Ledger: the collected fee is recorded separately from merchandise and shipping on the receipt (same receivable/liability journal, separate component), offsets the Mengantar COD cost in contribution, and is excluded from Purchase value and commission basis. (5) Refund: the fee is a buyer-paid fee allocated to the parcel's units by stable largest remainder at checkout; a refunded unit refunds its frozen share, so a full return refunds the whole fee (spec 11 conservation rule); a refused/uncollected parcel collects and refunds nothing.
