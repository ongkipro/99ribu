**Owner's three corrections are confirmed in the renders:**
- **One primary button.** There is a single "Perbarui pembagian paket" button with no outline twin, followed by "Draf tersimpan." (`cf9ae9…-02`).
- **Service metadata removed.** The chooser no longer repeats "Pengiriman simulasi · …".
- **Distinct captions.** The three Rp54.000 options read "Pontianak: 2 paket", "…dalam paket terpisah" and "…bersama produk lain" (`85b19a…-02/03`).

**Transfer from the references.**
- **Hierarchy and shell match S08:** focused 3-part header, step order, compact amount disclosure, and form-left/summary-right with a bounded neutral panel.
- **Density is intentionally higher than S08**, because real multi-origin parcels, contents and COD values replace S08's invented estimate and subsidy.
- **No template signals:** no dark hero, eyebrow pills, icon-tile card rows, decorative monospace, arrow buttons or unsourced claims. The palette stays Inter/purple, and yellow is unused here.
- **Imagery:** S08 had "Foto" slots in the summary. The renders have no product thumbnails, which is acceptable for checkout under the direction, so it is not a finding.

**Research gap.** The populated multi-origin chooser has no inspected external reference. Tokophi only proves the empty state, and the brief admits the chooser rests on CO5 and owner evidence. That is acceptable as a stated decision, but it is not reference fidelity.

This is visual acceptance only. It is not interaction or accessibility evidence, and design-gate was not run.

Most important change: make the chooser's fee use the same meaning of "ongkir" as the parcel card and the total (net of subsidy, or label it "tarif" and show the subsidy), so buyers compare the amount they will actually pay.
