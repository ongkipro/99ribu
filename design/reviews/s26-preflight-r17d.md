## Findings, most important first

1. **The allocation chooser breaks the hierarchy.**
   - In `allocation-changed-390-01…08` and `shipping-service-changed-390-01…09`, the disclosure opens eight options, and each one is 3–4 parcel paragraphs of about 6 lines like "Paket 1 dari Pontianak: Contoh Kaos (Satuan) × 2 pilihan; isi per pilihan: … Layanan lain: …". The page reaches about 6000px.
   - Two options carry the identical headline "3 paket · Rp54.000 ongkir", and a third shares "Rp54.000"; the only differences are buried mid-sentence.
   - At 320/200% (`shipping-two-origins-text-200-320-02…05`) the single *selected* option takes about 3 viewports. That is because the radio indent narrows the text column to about 230px.
   - The same contents are then repeated in the parcel cards directly below.
   - Consequences: the buyer cannot compare options, which hurts task clarity, and readability at enlarged text suffers.

2. **Wrong ongkir figure in the subsidized state.**
   - `shipping-subsidized-390-01` headlines the selected option "1 paket · Rp27.000 ongkir".
   - The parcel card (`-02`) shows Tarif Rp27.000, Subsidi −Rp3.000, Ongkir tersimpan **Rp24.000**, and the total is Rp222.000 = 198 + 24.
   - "ongkir" means two different amounts on one screen, which breaks CO5's buyer-paid/subsidy clarity.

3. **Pending service state still shows the old charge.**
   - In `shipping-two-origins-nojs-390-02`, the selected option text still says "Ekonomi simulasi · Rp27.000 … Rp8.000" under "2 paket · Rp35.000 ongkir".
   - Meanwhile `-03` shows Reguler checked for both parcels with "Biaya paket menunggu perhitungan baru".
   - The builder says stale parcel charges are hidden. They are hidden in the cards but still asserted in the chooser.

4. **The changed control is buried below open alternatives.**
   - In `shipping-service-changed-390`, the pending warning sits at the top, then 7 expanded options (slices 02–07). The parcel the buyer actually changed only appears in slice 07, roughly 4.5 screens down.
   - In `allocation-changed-390-01/02`, the first visible option is *unchecked* (Rp31.000) and the checked one (Rp35.000) sits inside the open list. That contradicts the stated "selected choice visible first".

5. **Minor issues:**
   - The focus ring is left on the note textarea in `contact-1024/1440` and `contact-text-200-collapsed-320-04`. That's a capture artifact, not a design issue, but it makes those frames less clean as evidence.
   - On payment 1024/1440 the column and shell edges don't line up (header link at x≈180, column at x≈400).
   - The accent yellow role from the accepted brand appears nowhere in the 63 renders. That's not a failure by itself, but please confirm it was meant to be absent from checkout.
