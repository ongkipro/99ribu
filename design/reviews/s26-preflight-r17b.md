## Material findings

1. **The allocation picker blocks the decision it asks for.** In `8e5a3421056895bc-shipping-two-origins-prepaid-390-02.png`, `715ceaa5864fc5fd-…-1440-01.png` and `bc73577d07d4f77c-…-cod-390-02.png`:
   - Options 4, 5 and 6 have the same text and the same fee.
   - Options 1/2 and 7/8 differ only in price.
   - The buyer cannot tell what they are choosing between.
   - At 390 the 8 cards take about two screens before Paket 1, so the parcel and service detail is pushed below the fold.
   - The ordinal prefixes satisfy the "distinct label" rule technically, but they do not make the options understandable.

2. **No-JS shows quote drift silently.**
   - `0db59506b59187b0-shipping-two-origins-nojs-390-03.png`: Reguler is checked in both parcels (Rp10.000 and Rp30.000), but "Ongkir tersimpan" reads Rp8.000 and Rp27.000 and the summary says Rp431.000. There is no warning.
   - `0827b3b1f34a7a20-review-two-origins-nojs-390-01.png`: the review total jumps to Rp436.000 with no "Total berubah…" alert.
   - The JS twin (`ad342a686746cd3d-…-prepaid-390-01.png`) does show "Total berubah dari Rp431.000 menjadi Rp433.000".
   - This breaks the contract rule that a quote change requires explicit review.

3. **The "restored" state cannot be told apart from "changed".** `2cbf7d6e95cf2f6a-shipping-service-restored-390-01..04.png` matches `29c075dfea781ef5-shipping-service-changed-390-*` at every visible point: the same stale banner, "Biaya paket menunggu perhitungan baru" and Rp431.000. Either restoring the service does not clear the stale state, or the capture does not show a restored state. That needs to be checked.
