| TY8 Private continuity | Guest session or `receipt_access`/`order_access` HttpOnly capability verified server-side before displaying order facts. Denied/unverified viewers see no order amounts, items or contact details. Reloading or revisiting `/orders/[orderId]` never creates a duplicate order/attempt or emits `Purchase`. |
