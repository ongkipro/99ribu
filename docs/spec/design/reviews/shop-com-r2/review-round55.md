## Reference transfer

- **Hierarchy.** The 99ribu home follows the reference's order and type scale: hero card, then "Kategori ›", then the shelf, the 96px switcher, the editorial rail and the footer (`894181…` vs `56ae2f…`). Section headings are 20px with a chevron, card text is 14/12 at 600, and the switcher label is 96/800. One mismatch: listings use a ~28px H1 "Semua produk" where shop.com uses an 18px "Results". It is defensible as the page H1.
- **Rhythm and density.** The 1250px container, the 7-card shelf, the 6-column grid and the 12–16px gaps match the reference grid. The phones keep shop.com's bleed and peek cues. The omitted rail, Save/Share/Buy now, ratings and merchant rows are correctly absent.
- **Imagery.** The category tiles (tint + white photo card instead of saturated full-bleed art) are a reasoned departure, because white-ground merchant photos cannot carry a white label. The departure is recorded.
- **Weakest transfer.** The banner rail puts the caption on white below a cut-out on a flat tint. Next to shop.com's photo-led cards with the caption on the image, it reads more like large product tiles than editorial. That treatment is a recorded coordinator decision, so it is not a finding.
- **Research.** Rationale exists for every deviation I checked (tiles, switcher medallion, banner, omnibox chips, dropdown, dock). The help and empty-cart desktop sections have references but no 99ribu render to compare against.

Most important change: add the "Paket Juara Mancing" ("7X Lebih STRIKE") and "Paket Borongan" ("BUBUK AJAIB · Reaksi Cepat") cover photos to `CLAIM_PHOTOS` so the home "Terbaru" shelf and `/promo` follow the accepted rule that curated surfaces leave claims out, then supply the missing `/bantuan` (390/1440), empty-cart 1440 and scrolled dock-320 renders.
