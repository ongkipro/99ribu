## Reference transfer

- **Hierarchy and rhythm:** these follow the shop.com home closely (`56ae…`, `44fc…`): pills → value card → Kategori › → shelf → switcher, with ~48px between sections. Skipped sections (stores, editorial) are justified by missing data, not invented.
- **Imagery:** this is where the transfer breaks. shop.com uses transparent cutouts and colourful art in pills and tiles. 99ribu uses white-ground marketplace photos, many heavy with text ("BUBUK AJAIB", "HYPER STRIKE"). Large cards hold up, but at 36px pills and tile-end crops they read as blank or noisy. The research notes this but never resolves it.
- **Density:** cards, grid and footer match the measured values: 14/18 title, 12/16 price, 20px radius.
- **Evidence gaps:**
  - `95b4…` (signed in, 390) is cropped above the hero copy, so the "Lihat produk" CTA isn't visible.
  - The multi-option PDP pills are still unverified because no published multi-option product exists.
- **Not found:** none of the template signals listed in the brief (dark hero, eyebrow pills, `A · B` labels, invented stats, swappable business).

**Most important change:** Replace the white-ground photos in the 36px pills and the category tiles with product-centred crops, or with the department icon where no clean crop exists, so the category navigation shows the category instead of an empty white disc.
