**Model differences to settle in this slice**

- AdsBookCMS area handling is kecamatan-level (`mengantar-areas.ts`: one row per `district|city|province`); 99ribu is kelurahan-level with postal code.
- AdsBookCMS never sends a callback URL (`callbackUrl: ""`) and reconciles by hourly advice; 99ribu polls advice every 300 s up to 24 times (`ADVICE_INTERVAL_SECONDS`, `ADVICE_MAX_POLLS`) and sends a loopback callback URL.
- AdsBookCMS retries a failed AutoLaris create with a **new** `reff_id`; 99ribu replays the same one. 99ribu's way follows the official idempotency note; do not copy the AdsBookCMS retry.
