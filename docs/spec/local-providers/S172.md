## Goal

The owner switches, per payment-policy version, who pays (a) the AutoLaris VA/QRIS admin fee and (b) the COD service fee: the buyer or the store. Model taken from AdsBookCMS (`src/lib/payment-fee-policy.ts`, `PaymentSettings.tsx`, reference only).

## What exists (2026-11-04)

- Payment policy `kind: payment` (owner + MFA, versioned, `src/modules/settings/policies.ts`): `cod_fee_bps`, `manual_bank_accounts`, `enabled_channels`.
- COD fee: buyer-borne today, per parcel `ceil((merchandise + shipping − subsidy) × bps / 10000)`, frozen on quote and order (`payment_charge_amount`, `cod_fee_bps`) and enforced by `app.guard_checkout_quote_payment` (migration 0032) and the shipment/fulfillment charge guards (0044).
- AutoLaris fee: store-borne (DEC-008 (3)); 99ribu refuses a provider `amount`/`total` other than the requested amount; the admin fee posts as `provider_fee_expense` at settlement (`simulateAutolarisSettlement`).
- The buyer picks the VA bank or QRIS on the order page, per payment attempt, after the order exists. The fee depends on that choice, so it cannot be frozen on the quote.
- AdsBookCMS live evidence (PAY-2): AutoLaris bills exactly the requested amount and deducts its fee from it. The guide's sample (`total = amount + admin`) is not what the live account did.

## Mechanism

1. **Policy fields** (all optional; absent = today's behaviour): `payment_fee_bearer: "store" | "buyer"` (default `store`), `cod_fee_bearer: "buyer" | "store"` (default `buyer`), `autolaris_fees: { [channel]: { kind: "fixed", amount } | { kind: "bps", bps } }`. Write-time rule: `payment_fee_bearer = "buyer"` needs a fee row for all six AutoLaris channels (`PAYMENT_FEE_TABLE_INCOMPLETE`); a fee above Rp1.000.000 for an order makes that channel unavailable for it.
2. **COD, store-borne:** the quote freezes `cod_fee_bps = 0` and `payment_charge_amount = 0`; the guard reads the bearer from the same policy version (migration 0046 replaces the guard function only). The courier's COD fee is still deducted at remittance, as now.
3. **AutoLaris, buyer-borne:** at attempt creation, `payment_fee_amount` = fixed fee, or for basis points the largest `X − amount_due` with `X − ceil(X × bps / 10000) ≤ amount_due` (the buyer never pays more than the provider deducts; the store absorbs at most the rounding rupiah). Frozen on `payment_attempt` (column from migration 0046). The attempt's `expected_amount`, the payment operation, the provider event and the economic receipt all stay the order value; only the AutoLaris bill `autolaris_payment.requested_amount` is `expected_amount + payment_fee_amount` (guarded by 0047, with the fee checked against `app.autolaris_buyer_fee` for the channel at the attempt's creation time).
4. **Money:** the provider bills exactly the requested amount and deducts its fee. When advice (or finance's manual confirmation) reports the bill, the recorded collection is the bill minus the frozen fee, so receipts, refunds, Purchase events, commissions and the receivable reconciliation never see the fee; the settled observation keeps the bill (seal in 0048: observation = collection + fee). At settlement the statement's gross is the receipt and its provider fee is `admin − buyer fee` (store cost, posted as `provider_fee_expense`); a provider fee below the frozen buyer fee is refused at dispatch (`AUTOLARIS_FEE_MISMATCH`, attempt failed, no instruction shown); the settlement keeps `AUTOLARIS_FEE_VARIANCE` as a second guard. A reported bill at or below the fee stays an unknown observation for finance; manual confirmation refuses it (`AUTOLARIS_AMOUNT_BELOW_FEE`). A late or mismatched payment goes to recovery for the bill minus the fee (the bank kept the fee). A refund never returns the payment fee (it paid the bank); the order page says so.
5. **Frozen everywhere:** a switch only applies to quotes (COD) or attempts (AutoLaris) created after the new version is effective; existing orders and attempts keep their amounts.
6. **Screens:** Pengaturan → Pembayaran gets two switches with plain consequences ("Pembeli" / "Platform", each with its consequence) and the fee table; checkout and the order page show "Biaya admin" (amount per bank/QRIS before the buyer confirms) only when the buyer bears it; staff order and payment views show the bearer and fee.

## Out of scope

Real provider fees (S97), changing the COD rate itself, refunding payment fees.

## Test plan

- Policy: both switches, missing fee row refused at write, older versions unchanged.
- COD: buyer and store both ways, guard refuses a mismatched charge, an order quoted before a switch keeps its fee.
- AutoLaris buyer-borne: fixed and percentage (bill rule exact at boundaries, SQL equals TypeScript), attempt frozen, provider bill, refusal when the provider amount differs or its fee is below the buyer fee, settlement nets the fee, receipt unchanged. Covered since: a reported bill at or below the fee stays unknown; finance confirms the bill, below-fee amounts are refused, and the receipt stays the order value. Not yet covered: the guard on a forged fee at insert (no application path can produce one), the late-payment recovery path, and the Rp1.000.000 fee cap (needs an order above about Rp4 juta). Rates (owner 2026-11-04): QRIS 0,7%, VA BCA Rp6.500, other VA Rp3.000.
- Store-borne: today's assertions unchanged (`autolaris-payments`, `payment-methods`, PR-5).
- Browser: settings switches (owner + MFA), fee line on the order page per channel at 390/1440.

## Risks

Money path and a migration: R4 review bound to the final surface. The fee table is the store's own number; a provider change makes a visible variance, not a silent loss.
